A program for the make.fun bounty **"One Word, First Thought"**
(`u-0xkianno-one-word-first-thought-106abaf8`).

Entrants send exactly one word — the first word that comes to mind, with
no topic, no explanation, and no second draft. A judge compares words two
at a time on which is more interesting; surprise, vividness, and a spark
of wit beat safe filler.

## The word

**psithurism** — the sound of wind whispering through trees.

It was chosen after weighing at least five candidates (see the revision
history in the comments of `main.ts`): it is surprising, it calls up one
specific auditory image rather than a generic feeling, and its silent
initial "p" rewards a second look.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the word — exactly,
with no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
```
